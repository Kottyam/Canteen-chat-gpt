GoCanteen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

GoCanteen is a canteen management and food ordering application for participating organisations and their employees. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the GoCanteen application.

1. Information we process

  • Account and employee information such as employee ID, name and account/authentication information.
  • Food ordering information such as orders, order dates, quantities, prices, guest orders and billing/contribution information.
  • Canteen and administrative information needed to operate menus, order windows, billing and reports.
  • Technical information required for authentication, application operation, security and troubleshooting.

2. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and provide access to the appropriate employee or administrator features.
  • To process and maintain food orders, billing and contribution calculations.
  • To operate canteen menus, order windows, reports and administrative functions.
  • To maintain application security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Data storage and service providers

GoCanteen may use third-party infrastructure and services, including Supabase and Google/Firebase services, to provide authentication, database, hosting, application and related technical functionality. Information is processed only as necessary to provide these services and operate the application.

4. Sharing of information

GoCanteen does not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorised canteen administrators and the organisation operating the relevant GoCanteen account when needed for ordering, billing, administration and support. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect the security of the service.

5. Data retention and deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the relevant GoCanteen service, maintain billing and accounting records, provide support, and meet applicable legal or organisational requirements. Users may request deletion of account-related personal information through the organisation or canteen administrator operating their GoCanteen account, subject to records that must be retained.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's privacy

GoCanteen is intended for use by employees and authorised users of participating organisations and is not directed to children.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning a GoCanteen account, contact the organisation or canteen administrator that provided your GoCanteen account. You may also use the developer contact information shown on the GoCanteen Google Play listing.
