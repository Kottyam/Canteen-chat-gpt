GoCanteen Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: 21 September 2026

GoCanteen is a canteen management and food ordering application for participating organisations. The canteen owner or administrator manages the canteen account and the employee/member accounts associated with that canteen.

How to delete a GoCanteen account and associated data

  1. Sign in to GoCanteen using the authorised canteen owner/administrator account.
  2. Open the canteen administration/account settings area.
  3. Use the Delete Canteen option to request or perform deletion of the canteen and its associated data, where available.

Deleting an individual employee/member

A canteen administrator can remove an individual employee/member from the canteen through the administration features. When an employee/member is removed, the associated account/member data is deleted or removed from active use subject to records that must be retained for billing, accounting, security or legal purposes.

What may be deleted

  • Canteen account and administration information.
  • Employee/member profile and account information associated with the deleted account.
  • Menus and operational information associated with the deleted canteen, where applicable.
  • Other associated application data that is eligible for deletion.

Data that may be retained

Some information may need to be retained for a reasonable period for billing, accounting, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal compliance or other legitimate organisational requirements. Retained information is not used for purposes unrelated to those requirements.

If you cannot access the app

If you cannot access GoCanteen and need an account or associated data deletion request, contact the organisation or canteen administrator that provided your GoCanteen account. You may also use the developer contact information shown on the GoCanteen Google Play listing and provide the canteen/account details needed to identify the request.

Important

Deleting a canteen is a significant administrative action and may remove access to the canteen's associated application data. Only an authorised canteen administrator should perform this action.
